Supporting The Dove

The Dove is run by the St Mary's Kington Parochial Church Council (PCC), so gifts to The Dove are made through the parish.

Donations are made on the Parish Giving Scheme website, a separate organisation that handles regular giving to parishes across the country. When you choose the button below you will leave this site and arrive on the Kington St Mary page there.
