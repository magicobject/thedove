What's on at The Dove

Our groups and activities are still being finalised. Here is the current draft — do get in touch if you have a suggestion for a group or event The Dove could host.

Weekly sessions

  • Tuesday10.00–12.00

    Music @ The Dove

    Join our orchestra of enthusiastic musicians, led by Julie Read.

  • Tuesday2.00–3.00

    Marches Voices

    A Capella choir singing madrigals and other unaccompanied songs. No audition, but sightreading an advantage.

  • Wednesday10.00–12.00

    Morning Coffee

    Join us in The Dove for coffee, cake and a chat. 4th Wednesday includes a Jigsaw Swap. All welcome!

  • Thursday10.00–12.00

    Toddle Time

    Soft play and cake.

Monthly meetings

  • 1st Friday

    Pause Café

    Enjoy good coffee, fresh cake and French conversation. All levels welcome.

  • Monthly

    Soul Reader book club

    Focusing on Christian books. All welcome. November's book: C. S. Lewis, 'Mere Christianity'.
